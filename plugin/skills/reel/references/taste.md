# Taste

## One bold idea, everything else plain

Pick one thing for the reel to do that a template wouldn't, and spend the effort there: a shape
that keeps time and changes form at every cut, the product's own UI rebuilt and driven, a real
number that fills the frame, the logo taken apart into the beat. Everything around it is plain
type on a flat ground, aligned to one edge. A reel where every element is decorated reads as
generated; one rich thing among plain ones reads as designed.

Write the idea in one sentence at the top of SPEC.md. If you can't, there isn't one yet.

## What makes a reel look generated, and what to do instead

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| Centred text on a gradient | Type aligned to one edge on a flat colour, on a grid. A gradient only if the brand has one. |
| Everything fading in | Things arrive by moving: a mask, a spring, a wipe. Fades are for exits and small things. |
| The logo at the end because reels have one | The logo where it means something, or not at all. |
| Warm cream, a serif display face, a terracotta accent | The subject's own palette. Without one: a near-black or near-white ground and one accent that earns its place. |
| Invented stats, quotes or users | The brief's words and the site's words, as they are. |
| Letter-spaced uppercase labels over every heading | Hierarchy from size and weight. |
| 01 / 02 / 03 on things that aren't steps | Numbers only on a real sequence. |
| Tag pills, fake UI chrome, live clocks, coordinates | Show the real product, or nothing that pretends to be data. |
| Glow, drop shadows, glass, particles, lens flares | Flat colour and type. Ornament has to encode something. |
| Manifesto copy: "Built for the future of work" | Flat, factual lines: what it is, what it does, who it's for. |
| Three typefaces, six colours | One family in two weights; four colours at most. |
| Emoji as decoration | Words. |

## Type

The site's own face when `site_kit` found one on Google Fonts; otherwise one plain face in two
weights. Big: headlines 96–160 px, nothing under 48 px that has to be read. Short lines, five
words or fewer, broken where the sense breaks.

## Pacing

One idea per section. A section stays until its words can be read twice. Cut on the beat. The
first frame is already the reel: no slow fade up from black, no title card.
